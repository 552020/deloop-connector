# Catalogue

One section per category, one subsection per level. The text under a level is what the agent may say at that level; above the released level it says only that there is more. Leave "not given" where nothing is filled. A line "archive:" points the owner side to the full values; it never reaches a room. Under each category, a line says what its levels mean; it stays here and never reaches a room. Level 0, nothing, is what the mandate grants until the person raises it. Passwords, tokens, keys and account data have no category: never.
